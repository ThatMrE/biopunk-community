Community = the movement

The best biotech founders are hidden in labs and universities — invisible to capital until it's almost too late. Biopunk finds them earlier and gives them a house, a lab, and a check to start.

We're not an accelerator with a Slack channel. We're a real-world network of co-living houses, shared wet labs, grants, hackathons, and Tuesday dinners — wired together by a community that backs people, not just decks. Everything in the directory above is a way in.
