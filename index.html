The path, end to end

The movement, mapped as a process — from the earliest idea and learning through funding, building, and late-stage deployment, validation, and regulatory guidance. Every door below is a link out.

Stage 03

Fund

First non-dilutive grants for an experiment, then a pre-seed check to go full-time.

Microgrants

Primordia Grants

primordiagrants.com

Fast microgrants — up to $3,000 — for early-stage biology experiments in community labs. No institution or credentials required; grantees share their work in the open.

Visit →
Capital

Biopunk VC

biopunkvc.com

A $25M pre-seed residency fund. A founder-friendly $10K first check, aggressive follow-on for the best, and pre-incorporation access to the people building what's next.

Visit →
Stage 04

Build & incubate

Live, build, and run real experiments in a shared wet lab alongside your cohort.

Houses

Biopunk House

biopunk.house

Co-living, co-working homes for scientist-founders. 12–15 builders per cohort, living and building side by side for a 3–4 month residency in cities chosen for their science.

Visit →
Labs

Biopunk Lab

biopunklab.com

The world's largest open-access biolab. Shared BSL-2 wet-lab infrastructure, tools, and training so founders can run real experiments on day one — no million-dollar setup.

Visit →
Stage 05

Deploy, validate & govern

Late stage — take it into the field, validate with rigorous testing, and get the stewardship and guidance to deploy responsibly.

Open Hardware

Crab Lab

crablaboratory.com

Open-source, modular shipping-container laboratories built to deploy anywhere. Blueprints and protocols are fully open, so any community can stand up a lab from local materials.

Visit →
Validation

Temple Lab

templelab.org

Analytical chemistry with harm reduction as its central sacrament — HPLC-anchored testing and full certificates of analysis for peptides, supplements, and research groups.

Visit →
Stewardship

Biological Frontier Foundation

biologicalfrontierfoundation.org

The nonprofit backbone of the community — stewardship and guidance for open, responsible biology, and support for the people and projects working at the frontier of science.

Visit →
Community = the movement

The best biotech founders are hidden in labs and universities — invisible to capital until it's almost too late. Biopunk finds them earlier and walks them from first idea to the clinic.

We're not an accelerator with a Slack channel. We're a real-world network of co-living houses, shared wet labs, grants, hackathons, and Tuesday dinners — wired together by a community that backs people, not just decks. Every stage above is a way in.
