The path, end to end

The movement, mapped as one continuous process — eleven stages from play and learning through building and financing to clinic, testing, and regulation. Every stop is a project you can walk into.

Learn

Grants

Fund the first experiment with fast, non-dilutive money.

Microgrants

Primordia Grants

Fast microgrants — up to $3,000 — for early-stage biology experiments in community labs. No institution or credentials required; grantees share their work in the open.

Incubate

The world's largest open-access biolab. Shared BSL-2 wet-lab infrastructure, tools, and training to incubate your project — real experiments from day one, no million-dollar setup.

Community = the movement

The best biotech founders are hidden in labs and universities — invisible to capital until it's almost too late. Biopunk finds them earlier and walks them from play to the clinic.

We're not an accelerator with a Slack channel. We're a real-world network of games, classes, shared wet labs, grants, hackathons, houses, and a fund — wired together by a community that backs people, not just decks. Every stage above is a way in.
